**Pattaya and Bangkok Advise 2026 (From Paul's Trip)**

Paul spent roughly a week in Pattaya followed by about 5 days in Bangkok. He focused on bars, go-go clubs, gentlemen's clubs, and Nuru-style massage places. He avoided using apps like TF on this trip.

Returned later and made eye contact with cuties, including one with very perky breasts, but another girl latched on.

At <a href="https://maps.app.goo.gl/UnX2BKNLpytNCM6ZA">3 Angels Bar</a> a lively girl grabbed him. Bar fine 1500 baht. She charged nothing for long-time beyond 4 extra drinks (720 baht total). He still gave her 2000 baht. Another night at a different Soi 6 bar: fun time, but bar fine quoted at 2000 baht and short-time over 3000 baht (he found this steep).

He preferred mainstream places.
- <a href="https://maps.app.goo.gl/EMVe3ZJFHhHqHRS47">Nuru Spa Pattaya</a> (Pattaya 3rd Road): Near his hotel. Paid 3000 baht for 1-hour soapy massage. Mamasan recommended a young girl with braces not on the daily list. Decent massage and good time overall.
- Other notes: Planned Kings and Nuru House but details limited. Mentioned past good experiences at Maggie Mays and High Rollers. Last year visited Sabai Dee (fishbowl lineup with color-coded price tags; green for younger/prettier and more expensive). Advised picking a girl who smiles and shows interest rather than just the prettiest.

**Advice from Pattaya**
- Soi 6 prices have risen (bar fine often 1000-2000, short-time 2000-3000, plus drink minimums). Long-time can offer better value at night. Short-time works better earlier in the day.
- Treat girls with respect and be friendly. It leads to better experiences and memorable times, even if you are not young or especially good-looking.
- Stunners can look great but sometimes give less enthusiastic service. Average-looking girls often provide better girlfriend experience.
- For freelancers: Walking Street nightclubs after about 2 am (many bar girls go there after shifts).
- Solo travel tip: Make friends with other travelers, expats, and staff. Return to the same places to build familiarity. Take a girl whenever you feel ready. No fixed schedule.
- Gentlemen's clubs can be good value if you accept that many women are past their peak.

**Advice from Bangkok**
- Most bar and go-go activity is takeaway (to short-time hotel or your hotel). Rooms are rarely inside the bars.
- Nana Plaza can be chaotic and packed. Some places (like Lollipop) have aggressive staff - leave if it ruins the mood.
- For first-timers to Thailand overall, Pattaya is easier and more straightforward than Bangkok.
- Nuru places vary by individual girl and day. Asking the mamasan for a recommendation can work well. Punch cards exist at some (J-Spot, Amor) linking multiple spas.
- Hotels in Thailand are generally relaxed about guests.
