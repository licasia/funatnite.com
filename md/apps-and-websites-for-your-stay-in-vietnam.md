# Preparation
- ***E-visa***: evisa.gov.vn (this is the official one, don't fall for any of the scam websites)
- ***E-visa agents***: <a href="https://lynnvisa.vn" rel="nofollow">lynnvisa.vn</a> or find agents in <a href="https://www.facebook.com/groups/vietnamvisaadvice" rel="nofollow">Vietnam Visa Advice group</a> on Fb (they can expedite applications or handle special cases)
- ***Fast track pass for Saigon airport immigration clearance***: <a href="https://www.klook.com" rel="nofollow">klook.com</a> (search for "Tan Son Nhat Fast Track Service" to find providers)

# Transportation
- ***Ride-hailing apps***: Grab, Xanh SM, Be
- ***Long-distance bus***: Vexere
- ***Local bus***: Busmap.vn

# Money
- ***QR payments***: <a href="https://moretapay.com" rel="nofollow">Moreta Pay</a> (this app allows you to link your credit card and then pay by QR code, which is widely accepted)
- ***Banks***: Vietcombank, Sacombank, Timo Bank (note, it's possible to open a bank account on a tourist visa, but you won't get a ATM card)

# Communication
- ***Apps***: Zalo (wide-spread), Fb (also wide-spread), Instagram (younger crowd), Threads (trending)
- ***Carriers***: Viettel (a bit more expensive, better coverage) and Mobifone (a bit cheaper, less coverage, SIM expires fast if not regularly topped up)
- ***Dating***: Tinder, Badoo, Bumble, Fb Dating, Tantan (90% freelancers & ladyboys) - don't limit yourself to just one, use them all

# Translation
- ***Apps***: Google Translate (no censorship), ChatGPT (some censorship), Gemini (little censorship), Grok (very little censorship)
