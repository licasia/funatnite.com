## Types of Barbershops in Vietnam

In general, there are three types of barbershops ("hớt tóc" in Vietnamese) in Vietnam.
1. ***The VIP or Korean barbershop***: this is the type of barbershop that is often featured in YouTube videos, because the service personnel usually consists exclusively of very pretty girls. They offer almost everything except for actual haircuts. This includes massage, shaving, facial and ear cleaning. You can think of them as a type of male spa. The girls are almost always cute & sexy, so it would be very tempting to think that you can also get more. Unfortunately, that is not the case. These establishments are 100% clean.
2. ***Local Vietnamese barbershops***: these are actual barbershops where you can get a real haircut or a shave for as low as just a couple of dollars. No extras here, either.
3. ***"Fake" barbershops***: these places are what many guys are after. The barbershop is often just a front and they function more like blowjob bars or massage parlors where they give you happy ending. Depending on the barbershop and the girl, you may also be able to get FS.

## How to recognize the fake barbershops
This is a bit difficult. There is never any obvious signage that gives them away. The one exception is the barbershops on the west end of <a href="https://maps.app.goo.gl/2UgCXUgr9HtAZoqK6" target="_blank" rel="nofollow">Nguyễn Phi Khanh Street</a> (aka NPK) in D1 of Saigon. If you walk along the street after dark, you will usually see a guy sitting outside. They will often call out to any male that looks like a foreigner.
